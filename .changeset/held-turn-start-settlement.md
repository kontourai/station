A Session whose turn start may have reached its engine no longer refuses every later message until Station restarts when that engine is observed gone. Contracts add to `@kontourai/station-contracts/orchestration` the refusal codes a send gets while an earlier start blocks it, so clients can say what clears it instead of suggesting a retry: `TURN_START_HELD_CODE` (`turn_start_held`: the next send settles it once the engine is seen to end), `TURN_START_HELD_UNTIL_RESTART_CODE` (`turn_start_held_until_restart`: the engine cannot report ending, so it stays held until the turn ends or Station is restarted) `TURN_START_BUSY_CODE` (`turn_start_busy`: another start has not finished) and `CONVERSATION_PREDECESSOR_ENGINE_LIVE_CODE` (`conversation_predecessor_engine_live`: a continuation refused while the failed predecessor's engine process still runs). Shared adds to `@kontourai/station-shared/runtime-event-projection` `TURN_ENGINE_ENDED_MESSAGE` (the interrupted-turn banner when Station settles such a start at runtime), `TURN_ENGINE_ENDED_CONTINUE_PROMPT` and `turnInterruptedContinuePrompt(reason)` (Continue after that banner does not claim a restart), and `TURN_COMPLETED_AFTER_INTERRUPTION_CODE` (the `runtime.warning` shown when a turn recorded as interrupted completes after all).
