# Code of Conduct

Station contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to make
participation safe, respectful, and constructive.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate and assume good faith while still discussing technical risks
  directly.
- Critique ideas and changes, not people.
- Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds, identities, and viewpoints.
- Keep reviews actionable and accept correction without retaliation.
- Protect private, security-sensitive, and personally identifying information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hello@kontourai.io`. Include links or
other context that can help the maintainers understand what happened. Reports
will be handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may edit or remove
contributions, issue warnings, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban participants when necessary to protect the community.

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, project
events, and other spaces where someone is acting on behalf of Station.
