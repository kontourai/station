---
name: stress-test-idea
description: Ask adaptive questions to clarify an idea, its constraints, and its unresolved decisions.
---

Ask the user for the idea they want to develop. Interview them in short rounds,
using their answers to choose the next questions. Offer recommendations with
reasons when useful, and let the user supply their own answer. Keep asking until
the important decisions are settled or the user chooses to stop. Do not invent
answers or take implementation actions. Keep unsettled decisions visible.
